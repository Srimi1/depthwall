# DepthWall identity

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The final master is `depthwall-logo.png`; Android uses the same master with adaptive-icon padding. The monochrome icon is a simplified vector companion.

Prompt: A premium AMOLED wallpaper app launcher icon. Pure black background. A bold papaya-orange geometric D formed by interleaving orbital bands around a circular negative-space opening. Planetary orbits and layered depth; clear at small sizes; no text or watermark.

Palette: black `#000000`, surface `#121212`, papaya `#FF870A`, ivory `#F4EEE5`.
