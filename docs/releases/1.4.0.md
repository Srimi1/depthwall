# DepthWall 1.4.0

Fixes Super Wallpaper packs that crash, upgrades the built-in live scenes, and adds 12 wallpapers. It installs as a normal update over 1.3.0.

## Super Wallpaper packs

On Android 17, only the Moon pack worked. Earth, Mars, Saturn, Snow Mountain and Geometry are built on an old version of the Unity engine, which crashes on 64-bit Android 11 and later. Moon uses a different engine.

Running these packs as 32-bit apps avoids the crash. To set one up:
1. Uninstall the pack that doesn't work.
2. In DepthWall, open **Live → Original Xiaomi / HyperOS packs** and tap **Install pack as 32-bit**.
3. Choose the pack's APK file in Downloads.

DepthWall removes the pack's 64-bit engine files, signs the pack on your phone and opens Android's installer. Installed packs then show **Running as 32-bit** on their card.

- Your phone must support 32-bit apps. If it doesn't, DepthWall tells you.
- Packs installed this way update only through DepthWall.

## Live scenes

- **Earth, Mars and Moon** start as a whole planet seen from orbit and zoom to the horizon when you unlock. Earth has oceans, clouds, an atmosphere glow and city lights.
- **Saturn** has lit rings and shadows.
- **Snow** has moonlit peaks, fog and falling snow.
- **Geometry** shows glowing 3D rings on black.
