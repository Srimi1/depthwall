# DepthWall 1.2.0

Fixes Super wallpapers that stay black or frozen after a phone restart, and adds a collection made for the OnePlus 7T Pro McLaren's 1440 × 3120 AMOLED panel. Android 12 or newer is required.

## Install: uninstall the old app first

1.2.0 is signed with a **new signing key** because the key used for earlier versions was lost. Android cannot install it over 1.0.1 or 1.1.0. Uninstall DepthWall (and DepthWall Preview, if you have it), then install `DepthWall-1.2.0.apk`. This removes the old app's favourites, downloads, imports and settings once. Wallpapers already applied to your screens stay until you change them. Every later version will install as a normal update over 1.2.0.

## After a restart

- **DepthWall originals recover on their own.** If the graphics surface cannot be created while the phone is still starting up, the wallpaper retries with backoff. If the surface is lost, it is rebuilt rather than retried forever. If a depth layer cannot be read, that layer is drawn black and the rest keeps working.
- **Apply again to restart.** Applying the active DepthWall wallpaper again now restarts its engine. Android does not create a new engine in that case. It only sends the running one a command, which DepthWall now handles.
- **Xiaomi/HyperOS ports.** Applying a port again cannot restart it, for the same reason. When a port is your current wallpaper, its card shows **Black or frozen? Restart it**. DepthWall switches that screen to plain black so Android unbinds the stuck port, offers its App info page for a force stop, and then opens the picker. Choose **Home and lock**. DepthWall can't fix crashes inside the port apps, so **Use DepthWall original** stays available.
- **One scene per pack.** DepthWall warns when Home and Lock use two scenes from the same port pack. They run in one process and crash together at startup.
- Before you enter your PIN after a restart, Android shows its default image for live wallpapers. That is normal, and your choice returns after unlocking.

## Best for your phone

A new row at the top of Explore collects wallpapers suited to this panel:

- eleven new originals: Carbon Weave, Papaya Horizon, Edge Glow, Papaya Eclipse, Rise, Ninety, Carbon Chamfer, Papaya Orbit, Ember Nebula, Aero Contours and Midnight Weave;
- favourites from the existing set: Carbon Fold, Papaya Racing Line, Papaya Apex, Afterburn and Velocity;
- the live Sun scene.

The new designs keep the clock area and status bar true black, stay clear of the curved edges, and leave unused AMOLED pixels off. They contain no logos, marque shapes or brand names. They are also listed under the **Papaya Edition** and **Space** filters.

## Validation and limits

CI built the debug, preview and Android test APKs and ran the unit tests and lint. Instrumentation tests were compiled but not run. The restart behaviour follows crDroid 17.0 framework source and has not been checked after a real reboot on the phone yet. To help confirm it, run `tools/device_report.sh` right after a reboot and unlock. See [After a restart](../COMPATIBILITY.md#after-a-restart) and [troubleshooting](../INSTALL.md#black-or-frozen-after-a-restart).
