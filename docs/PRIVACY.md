# Privacy and content

DepthWall has no analytics, advertisements, account system or subscription. Imported images, favourites, scene settings and APK inspection results remain on the device. Imported APKs are opened only by Android's package installer after the user's action.

Online searches contact NASA, Wikimedia Commons or Wallhaven directly. ESA, ISRO, JAXA and CSA collections are searches on Commons, not official agency feeds. Website import contacts the URL you provide, its public redirect destinations and image hosts; previews also contact image hosts. These services receive ordinary network information such as request queries and IP addresses. Opening an original-pack source link launches the user's browser, where that site's own policies apply. No image or private phone content is uploaded by DepthWall.

Downloads preserve the source's creator and licence information. NASA media may include third-party credits; the source page remains available from the wallpaper details. Wikimedia entries are restricted to returned Creative Commons/public-domain licence metadata. Wallhaven and website imports retain source links but do not imply a free license; check creator terms. The user is responsible for the rights to personally imported files. No subscription or download-count quota is imposed by DepthWall. External service rate limits still apply.

Bundled generative artwork and the application identity are original to this project. Xiaomi, HyperOS, linuxct, Ouxyl, crDroid, OnePlus and McLaren names identify compatibility targets or third-party creators; this project is not affiliated with them.
